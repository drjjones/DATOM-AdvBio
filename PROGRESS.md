### 2026-09-18: Unit 1, water and the chemistry of life, built

Jordan on the redesign: "the style looks good but the content is missing." Unit 1 is now built to the unit page spec, five standards, each a section with a hook sentence over the live visual, the model's controls, an explanation of at most 150 words (counted by `python3` in the build notes), three questions with instant feedback including one prediction answered by working the model, and the "I can" checklist. Sections are deep linkable (`#b1-2`), each has "Present this section," and in presentation mode a section reveals its parts one arrow press at a time before the deck moves on.

- **Infrastructure.** `src/lib/mol/viewer.ts` is the carbon viewer generalized: any structure with per-atom colors, radii, tap labels, dashed links, faces, and decorations; `replace`, `setPositions`, `morph`, `recolor`. `src/lib/mol/zmatrix.ts` builds Cartesian coordinates from bond lengths, angles, and dihedrals, so molecules come from published geometry with no data files. `src/lib/ui.ts` (sliders, segmented controls, toggles, readouts, all 44px), `src/lib/sim/timeline.ts` (ring buffer plus scrubber), `src/lib/sim/chart.ts` (hairline line chart that follows the environment), `src/scripts/cfu.ts` (questions, remembered per device), `src/scripts/parts.ts` (part-by-part presenting), `src/components/StandardSection.astro`. Content lives in `content/units/unit01.json`; models in `src/units/u01/`, code-split so other units do not load them.
- **B1.1 Bond explorer.** Nine bonds (H to H through Na to Cl, plus water to water). Bohr shells of both atoms, the electronegativity difference on a live zoned scale, the molecule in 3D from standard geometry colored by partial charge with its dipole arrow, or by element. Carbon dioxide is included on purpose: polar bonds, straight shape, nonpolar molecule.
- **B1.2 Hydrogen bond simulation.** 84 two-dimensional dipoles with short-range repulsion, a hydrogen-bond spring that weakens with temperature, thermal kicks, and orientation torque. Six modes: cohesion (surface molecules outlined, bonds per molecule surface vs interior), adhesion (a polar tube; narrower rises higher), specific heat (a heater; water warms slower than a liquid without hydrogen bonds, charted), evaporative cooling (the fastest molecules escape; the temperature of what remains falls, charted), solvent (a salt crystal dissolves into hydration shells; nonpolar blobs are pushed together), ice (below zero the molecules lock to an open lattice and the measured density falls, with the real 0.92 quoted beside it). Temperature slider, play/pause, timeline scrubber over the last 900 steps.
- **B1.3 pH and buffers.** Left beaker draws hydrogen ions as dots relative to neutral water for any pH, so each step is visibly ten times. Right: drops of acid or base (0.5 mmol into 1 L) into pure water and into a bicarbonate buffer (24 mM HCO3-, 1.2 mM H2CO3, pKa 6.1, Henderson-Hasselbalch), both traces on one chart with the blood band 7.35 to 7.45; strong or weak acid for the water beaker; a hundred-molecule strip comparing HCl and acetic acid at 0.1 M.
- **B1.4 Carbon.** A two-carbon skeleton carrying any of seven functional groups, in 3D with the group in color and the skeleton in gray, with polarity, charge at pH 7, behavior in water, and where the group is found. A dehydration synthesis and hydrolysis step-through on a slider. The Miller and Urey apparatus in four steps with "showed" and "did not show" spec lines.
- **B1.5 Macromolecules.** One stage, four families: glucose ring (chair) with alpha and beta chain figures; palmitic (16:0) and oleic (18:1 cis-9) acids as three packed chains, with a bilayer figure; an ideal alpha helix (100 degrees and 1.5 Å per residue) with i to i+4 hydrogen bonds that unfolds toward a random coil on a heat slider, with a folding figure; ten base pairs of B-DNA from fiber parameters (36 degrees, 3.38 Å rise, 8.9 Å phosphate radius, 150 degree strand offset) with tappable bases and the pairing ladder.
- **Verified.** Build green; console clean on all five standard pages and the unit page; the scripted check (`npm run check:unit`) answers questions, enters presentation mode, steps hook to controls to explanation to check to checklist to the next section, exits, and toggles the environment; without WebGL the three 3D stages show the notice and the two simulations still run, with no script errors; Lighthouse desktop 100 / 100 / 100 on the home, unit, and all five standard pages, 99 on the carbon page; house-voice sweep clean; every explanation at or under 150 words.
